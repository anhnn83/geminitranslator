🚀 How to use Gemini Translator

1. Get API Key

Get your free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.

2. Text Translation

Highlight any text on your screen, then press your configured Text Shortcut.

3. Image Translation (OCR)

Press your OS screenshot shortcut (Win+Shift+S on Windows, or Flameshot/Spectacle on Linux) to capture a region to your clipboard. Then, press your configured Image Shortcut.

💡 Pro Tip: Prevent Disk Clutter

Your OS might save every screenshot to your hard drive. To prevent this:
- Windows: Open Snipping Tool settings -> Turn OFF "Automatically save screenshots".
- Linux: Configure your screenshot tool to "Copy to Clipboard only" instead of saving to a file.
